# v219 decision trace and bounded completion review

Final review runs once after existing ranking and contracts. Up to five eligible candidates execute in cloned state. Record actual card, cash/income delta, projected final VP delta (not guaranteed future VP), residual cards and action budget. The audit shows selection and two alternatives, score reasons, card opportunity cost and separate search/verification statuses.

For development and loans, inspect up to six freshly legal builds after paying and discarding the preparation card. Development uses the current plan primary targets; loans may fund any real build. For sale-dependent goods, check an actual currently available sale using the residual hand and shared resources. No card, money or resource is reused. No recursion or permanent state mutation.

VERIFIED means a short sequence on the frozen board, NOT a complete strategic plan or guaranteed execution across an opponent turn. BUILD_ONLY means build succeeded but sale was not confirmed. Missing cards/cash/routes or distant preparation remains UNCONFIRMED, not illegal. No refill, hidden hands or settlement is added even with information options enabled. Existing information-aware planning remains unchanged.

Only demonstrable preparation deadline failure receives a bounded -24 development adjustment; cash/income/projected VP relief protects resource recovery. Era-I investments use remaining game actions (m1 has its era deadline). No blanket penalty for unknown follow-up, positive bonus, forced locomotive factory, new legality rule or competition bonus. Failure in sampled builds does not prove all future builds impossible. Existing exclusions are not a complete rejection census; audit distinguishes missing candidates from low evaluation.

First instrumentation stage only: no full-plan multi-turn proof, calibrated opponent interception or new strategy switching. Future comparisons should use identical saved positions with cheat OFF. Regression tests alone do not establish playing strength.
